About Us

About SpeechBloom AI

SpeechBloom AI was created to give multilingual children engaging, accessible, and thoughtfully designed pronunciation practice. By addressing unique Indian language acoustic features — including Hindi retroflex consonants, dental stops, aspirated consonants, and English–Hindi code-switching — SpeechBloom bridges the gap between formal sessions and consistent home practice.

🎯

Our Mission

Make daily speech practice joyful, measurable, and accessible to every Indian family.

🤖

The AI Engine

MFCC acoustic feature extraction with phonetic string alignment detects substitutions, distortions, and omissions in real time.

Data Privacy

  • Audio recordings are stored locally or in access-controlled Supabase Storage.
  • Pediatric speaker records are pseudonymous — no personally identifiable audio metadata retained.
  • Parents and guardians retain full authority to request data deletion at any time.
  • No recording is ever shared with third parties.
